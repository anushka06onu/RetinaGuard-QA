# Dataset Card: DeepDRiD (External Generalization Benchmark)

## Dataset Summary
- **Dataset Name:** DeepDRiD (Deep Diabetic Retinopathy Image Dataset Challenge)
- **Source Repository:** https://github.com/deepdrdoc/DeepDRiD
- **Associated Publication:** Liu et al., "DeepDRiD: Diabetic Retinopathy—Grading and Image Quality Estimation Challenge", Patterns / Cell Press 2022.
- **Design Role:** Zero-shot external test cohort to quantify cross-camera, cross-clinic domain shift without retraining.
- **Key Characteristics:** Multi-field fundus imaging (macula-centered and optic-disc centered) from diverse clinical cameras.
- **Quality Taxonomy:** Multi-level overall quality and regional usability flags.
